# Google Play App Growth & Market Opportunity Analytics

A Product Analytics / Growth Strategy case study using 2.3M+ Google Play apps.

## Business question
Where should an app developer, publisher or app-marketplace team prioritize investment to maximize the probability of meaningful scale?

## Core analysis
- Market/install distribution
- Category breakout analysis
- 100K+ penetration
- Opportunity scoring
- Competition vs breakout
- Rating/social-proof analysis
- Free vs paid adoption
- IAP adoption
- Developer portfolio depth

## Headline findings
- 77.6% of apps have <10K installs.
- 684 apps have 100M+ installs.
- Casino has the highest completed opportunity score (84.43).
- Simulation has the highest observed breakout rate (12.01%).
- 99.78% of 1M+ apps are free.
- IAP adoption is 45.47% among 1M+ apps vs 7.66% below 1M.
- 6–10 app developers have the highest observed breakout rate (3.15%).

## Strategic recommendations
1. Prioritize categories using breakout potential and competition, not category size alone.
2. Validate Casino, Role Playing, Weather, Simulation and Racing first.
3. Use free/low-friction acquisition for mass-market scale.
4. Build monetization into the product journey.
5. Use structured portfolio experimentation.
6. Strengthen ratings/reviews as a product growth loop.

## Limitation
The underlying dataset is historical (around 2021) and the analysis is observational. Revenue, CAC, retention and user-level engagement are unavailable.
